# Huddle naming + concept brief
# Paste this whole file to another AI. Ask it to invent names, not to write code.

You are a naming and product-language partner for **Freezer Penguin**, a freezer / fridge inventory app.

Do **not** write Flutter, SQL, or implementation. Do **not** replace the current Kitchen redesign preview. Invent **name systems** and short rationale only.

---

## Product in one paragraph

Freezer Penguin helps a household know what food they already have, what is about to expire, and what they can cook tonight. Brand is a penguin: icy blue, navy, orange. Voice is warm, a little expedition / colony flavored, never cute-to-the-point-of-unclear. The app is not a social network first. Cooking help is not medical or nutrition advice.

Three visual themes exist. **Arctic** may use colony / ice / expedition words. **Light** and **Dark** should stay plainer kitchen English. Every huddle name you propose MUST have an Arctic pair and a Light/Dark pair.

---

## What “the last tab” is

The last bottom tab is a **hub with three doors**, not one infinite feed and not a fifth “AI chat” tab.

Working labels today (placeholders — you may beat them):

| Role | Arctic (working) | Light/Dark (working) | What lives inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hub (the tab itself) | The Huddle | Cool Tips | Three doors only. No dump of posts on this screen. |
| Door 1 — social / advice | The Bulletin | Kitchen Talk | Posts, food tips, popular/public recipes, most-liked recipe. Save a recipe from here. |
| Door 2 — cook from stock | The Galley | From the Icebox | Full “what can I make with Soon / Urgent food” list. Same engine as Home’s top 3, but every match. |
| Door 3 — the user’s recipes | The Cookbook | My Recipes | Saved vs Created. Created recipes can be **Private** or **Colony** (public to the community). **Add recipe lives here.** |

The center **+** FAB on the main app is **add food only**. Recipes are never added from that button.

---

## Hard constraints (reject ideas that break these)

1. **Three rooms, not one feed.** The hub is a chooser. Each door is a distinct job.
2. **Not a 5th tab for AI.** AI is a named companion (Waddle / Pip / Scout / custom) opened from Home and from a recipe. Huddle is human/community + cook-from-stock + personal recipes.
3. **Theme pairs.** Arctic names can be penguin/expedition. Light names must still make sense to someone who hates themed copy. If Arctic is poetic, Light must be instantly understandable.
4. **Do not steal Home’s job.** Home is HQ widgets: Colony/Icebox Status donut (Urgent / Soon / Safe), Tonight’s Briefing (top 3 recipes), optional insights (top picks, bought-but-barely-eaten, most liked recipe, top tips). Huddle Door 2 is the *full* cook list, not a duplicate donut.
5. **No SQL/academic badges** on consumer copy (no SUBQUERY, WINDOW, etc.).
6. **Community is optional and kind.** Posts are food advice and public recipes, not a generic Instagram clone. Empty states should feel like a small colony, not a dead social product.
7. **Privacy is real.** Created recipes default private. Public = visible in Door 1. Do not name Door 3 something that implies everything is public.
8. **Names must be speakable** in a 4-tab bar and as an AppBar title. Prefer 1–3 words. Avoid puns that need a footnote. Avoid “Mess Hall” if it sounds like a cafeteria dump; Door 2 is “cook from *my* icebox.”
9. **Do not name the AI here.** Companion naming is a separate system (Waddle, Pip, Scout, custom).
10. **English first** for this exercise. Note if a name will be painful in Spanish or Cantonese later.

## Jobs to be named (give each a one-line job story)

- **Hub:** I want to pick *talk*, *cook*, or *keep* without mixing them.
- **Door 1:** I want tips and other kitchens’ public recipes, and I want to see what the colony liked most.
- **Door 2:** I want every recipe that uses food that is Urgent or Soon, not just tonight’s three.
- **Door 3:** I want my saved recipes and the recipes I wrote, and I want to add a new one.

Optional micro-labels inside doors (you may rename these too):

- Door 1: Most liked recipe, posts, Save
- Door 2: Uses N items from Soon list
- Door 3: Saved / Created, Private / Colony

## Current working names — why they exist (you may discard them)

- **Huddle / Cool Tips:** penguin cluster vs plain “tips.” Risk: Cool Tips sounds like Door 1 only, not a hub of three rooms.
- **Bulletin / Kitchen Talk:** newspaper + chatter. Risk: Bulletin is cold; Kitchen Talk undersells public recipes.
- **Galley / From the Icebox:** ship kitchen + literal. Risk: Galley is jargon; Icebox is strong.
- **Cookbook / My Recipes:** obvious. Risk: Cookbook is generic; Arctic loses flavor.

Previously used and **retired** (do not revive unless you have a strong reason): The Rookery, The Mess, The Nest, Community, Cook from my icebox. Rookery/Mess/Nest were cute but unclear.

## What to deliver

Produce **8 complete name systems**. Each system must include:

1. Hub name (Arctic + Light)
2. Door 1, Door 2, Door 3 (Arctic + Light)
3. One sentence: why this system hangs together
4. One risk: how it could confuse a first-time user
5. Score yourself 1–5 on: **clarity**, **brand**, **tab-bar fit**, **non-creepy social**

Then pick a **recommended system** and a **safe backup** (plain kitchen English).

Also give **10 rejected names** with one-line reasons (too cute, too military, too social-app, collisions with Home widgets, etc.).

Do not output code. Do not redesign Home. Do not invent a fourth door unless you argue it is necessary — default is three.
